VistaPlan Privacy Policy

Last updated: 9/30/26


VistaPlan ("VistaPlan," "we," "us," or "our") is a mobile app that helps groups plan trips together — shared itineraries, budgets, group chat, packing lists, and polls. This policy explains what information we collect when you use VistaPlan, how it's used, and the choices you have.

Information We Collect

Account information

When you create a VistaPlan account, we collect your name, email address, and password. Your password is handled by Firebase Authentication (our authentication provider) and is never stored by us in plain text or visible to us directly.

Trip content you and your trip members create

VistaPlan is built around sharing trip plans with a group. Anything you add to a trip is visible to every other member of that trip — that's the point of the app. This includes:

  • Trip names, destinations, and dates
  • Itinerary items, including any notes, estimated costs, and the names/addresses of places you add
  • Expenses, who paid, and how costs are split among the group
  • Messages sent in a trip's group chat
  • Packing list items
  • Polls you create and the votes you cast
  • Trip cover photos you choose to upload

Information sent to third-party services on your behalf

Some features work by sending a limited amount of information to outside services:

Service

What's sent

Geoapify

The destination name and category you're browsing (e.g. "restaurants near Tokyo"), used to find and display real places and attractions.

Wikimedia Commons

The approximate coordinates of a place you're viewing, used to look up a public photo of that location, where one exists.

These lookups aren't tied to your personal identity by these services — they're simple, anonymous API requests for place and photo data.

What we don't collect

VistaPlan does not use advertising SDKs, does not track you across other apps or websites, and does not request your device's precise location. We do not include any analytics or tracking SDK in the app.

How We Use Your Information

  • To create and operate your account
  • To let you create, join, and collaborate on trips with people you invite
  • To sync your trip data in real time across your devices and your trip members' devices
  • To look up attraction and restaurant information when you use Discover Attractions
  • To communicate with you about your account, if needed (e.g. password reset emails)

How Information Is Shared

With your trip members

Anyone you share a trip's invite code with — and anyone already on that trip — can see everything added to that trip: your name, your messages, expenses you've logged, and items you've added. This sharing is core to how VistaPlan works and is visible to you at all times within the app.

With service providers

We use the following infrastructure providers to operate VistaPlan. They process data on our behalf and are bound by their own privacy and security commitments:

  • Google Firebase (Authentication, Firestore database, and Cloud Storage) — hosts your account, trip data, and uploaded photos
  • Geoapify — powers attraction and restaurant search
  • Wikimedia Commons — provides public photos for places, where available

What we don't do

We do not sell your personal information. We do not share your data with advertisers or data brokers.

Your Choices and Rights

  • Access and update: you can view and edit your trip content directly in the app at any time.
  • Delete your account: you can permanently delete your account at any time from Settings → Delete Account. This removes your profile; any trips you own are deleted for everyone on them, and you're removed from any trips you've joined but don't own.
  • Leave a trip: you can leave any trip you've joined without deleting your account, from that trip's Trip Info screen.

Data Retention

We retain your account and trip data for as long as your account is active, or as needed to provide the app's features (for example, so other trip members can still see a shared trip). When you delete your account or a trip, the associated data is deleted from our active systems as described above.

Data Security

Data is encrypted in transit between the app and our servers. Access to trip data is restricted by server-side security rules that only allow a trip's own members to read or write that trip's content.

Children's Privacy

VistaPlan is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us at the address below and we will remove it.

International Users

VistaPlan's data is stored and processed using Google Firebase's infrastructure, which may store or process data in countries other than your own.

Changes to This Policy

If we make material changes to this policy, we'll update the "Last updated" date at the top of this page.
